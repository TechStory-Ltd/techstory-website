Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 August 2026

Who We Are

This Privacy Policy applies to mobile applications and websites operated by Tech Story Ltd, including applications that link to this policy.

Tech Story Ltd is registered in England and Wales. Company number: 15902066. Registered office: Crown House, 27 Old Gloucester Street, London, United Kingdom, WC1N 3AX.

Privacy contact: contact@techstory.org.uk.

Information We Collect

Our mobile applications are designed to work without requiring a user account. Unless a specific application screen, permission prompt, or store listing says otherwise, our apps do not collect names, email addresses, contacts, photos, precise location, payment information, or other sensitive personal data.

Some apps may offer subscription plans through Google Play. Purchases and payments are processed by Google Play, not directly by Tech Story. We do not receive or store your full payment card details.

To provide subscription features, an app may receive or check limited subscription information from Google Play, such as product identifier, purchase status, renewal status, transaction timing, and purchase token or order reference. This information is used only to verify access, prevent fraud, provide support, and comply with legal or accounting obligations.

If you contact us by email, we receive the information you choose to send, such as your email address, name, message, and any attachments.

Our website may receive standard technical information from your browser and hosting providers, such as IP address, user agent, requested page, and time of request. This information is used to provide, secure, and maintain the website.

Device Permissions

If an app requests access to device features such as camera, microphone, storage, Bluetooth, network access, or location, the app uses that access only to provide the feature shown in the app. We do not use device permissions for advertising or selling personal data.

How We Use Information

We use information only where needed to:

  • provide and operate our apps and website;
  • verify and manage subscription access purchased through Google Play;
  • respond to messages, support requests, and business enquiries;
  • debug, protect, and improve our services;
  • comply with legal obligations.

Sharing Information

We do not sell personal data. We may share limited information with service providers that help us operate our apps, website, email, hosting, security, or business systems. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights, users, or services.

Subscription purchases are handled by Google Play. Google may process payment and account information under Google's own privacy policy and payment terms.

Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we aim to limit access to information to people and providers who need it for the purposes described in this policy.

Data Retention and Deletion

We keep personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including support, legal, accounting, and security purposes.

If you want us to delete information you have sent to us, contact contact@techstory.org.uk. We will respond to deletion requests where required by applicable law.

Children

Our apps and website are not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data to us, please contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we make changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
